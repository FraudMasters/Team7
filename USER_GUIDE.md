## Introduction

### Who Is This Guide For?

This guide is written for **HR recruiters and hiring managers** at small-to-medium businesses who need to evaluate candidates efficiently and make better hiring decisions. Whether you're hiring one person or building an entire team, AgentHR helps you:

- **Save time** by automatically screening resumes
- **Find better matches** with AI-powered candidate ranking
- **Make informed decisions** with clear, explainable recommendations
- **Stay organized** with visual pipeline management
- **Collaborate effectively** with your hiring team

### What Is AgentHR?

AgentHR is an **AI-powered hiring platform** that helps you evaluate candidates faster and more accurately. Here's what it does:

#### **Resume Analysis**
Upload resumes in PDF or DOCX format, and AgentHR automatically extracts:
- Contact information
- Work experience
- Education history
- Skills and qualifications
- Certifications and achievements

#### **Smart Matching**
Compare candidates to your job openings using three intelligent methods:
- **Keyword Matching** – Finds exact skill matches
- **TF-IDF Matching** – Weights important skills more heavily
- **Vector Similarity** – Understands context and meaning (e.g., knows that "JS" and "JavaScript" are the same thing)

#### **AI-Powered Ranking**
Get ranked candidate lists based on 13 factors, including:
- Skills match ratio
- Experience relevance
- Education level
- Resume completeness
- And 9 more data-driven factors

#### **Recommendation Levels**
Each candidate receives a clear recommendation:
- **Excellent** (≥80%) – Top candidate, highly recommended
- **Good** (60-79%) – Strong match, recommend interview
- **Maybe** (40-59%) – Potential match, review needed
- **Poor** (<40%) – Not a match

#### **Visual Pipeline Management**
Track all candidates through your hiring process with a Kanban board:
- Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer → Hired (or Rejected)

#### **Continuous Improvement**
The more you use AgentHR and provide feedback, the smarter it gets. Your feedback helps improve recommendations for future hires.

### What Makes AgentHR Different?

| Traditional Hiring | AgentHR |
|-------------------|---------|
| Manually review every resume | AI ranks candidates automatically |
| Subjective screening | Data-driven, explainable rankings |
| Hours spent on unqualified candidates | Focus on top-rated candidates |
| Gut-feeling decisions | Clear scores with 13 contributing factors |
| No visibility into what works | Analytics show what's working |

### How This Guide Is Organized

This guide follows the **natural hiring workflow**:

1. **Get started** – Access the platform and learn the basics
2. **Post jobs** – Create job vacancies with clear requirements
3. **Upload resumes** – Add candidate resumes to the system
4. **Understand scores** – Learn how match scores and rankings work
5. **Evaluate candidates** – Use rankings and comparisons to shortlist
6. **Manage pipeline** – Track candidates through the hiring process
7. **Provide feedback** – Help the AI learn and improve
8. **Analyze results** – Use analytics to optimize your hiring

### Before You Begin

To get the most out of AgentHR, you'll need:

- **Job vacancies** – Clear descriptions of roles you're hiring for
- **Resumes** – Candidate resumes in PDF or DOCX format
- **Time to review** – While AI speeds up screening, you'll still want to review top candidates personally
- **Willingness to provide feedback** – Your input makes the system smarter

### Tips for Success

Here are some best practices before we dive in:

✅ **Be specific in job descriptions** – Detailed requirements lead to better matches

✅ **Upload resumes in batches** – Process multiple candidates at once for efficiency

✅ **Trust but verify** – Use AI rankings as a tool, not a replacement for human judgment

✅ **Provide feedback** – Tell AgentHR when recommendations are helpful (or not)

✅ **Use the Kanban board** – Keep your pipeline organized and visible

✅ **Review feature importance** – Understand which factors are driving rankings
